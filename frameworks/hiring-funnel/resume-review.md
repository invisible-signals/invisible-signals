# Résumé Review

## Goal of this phase

Market yourself and get an interview. The résumé does not get you the job — it gets you the next conversation.

## Audience

Recruiter or ATS filter, not the engineer who will work with you. At many companies, the résumé is reviewed by someone non-technical before it reaches the hiring manager.

## What the reviewer is looking for

- Does this candidate have experience relevant to the role?
- Do their listed skills match the job requirements?
- Can they communicate their work clearly and concisely?
- Is there a coherent trajectory?

## Visible signals

- Job titles and company names
- Technologies, languages, and frameworks listed
- Quality and specificity of bullet points
- Length and formatting

## Invisible signals

- Trajectory — is scope and responsibility growing over time?
- Judgment — what they chose to include and what they left out
- Ownership — do bullets describe participation or impact?
- Whether the résumé was tailored to this role or is generic

## Common mistakes

- Writing an objective statement that describes what you want instead of what you offer
- Bullet points that describe activity, not outcomes (*"Improved test coverage"* vs. *"Increased unit test coverage from 40% to 78%, reducing regression incidents by half"*)
- Claiming "Expert" across a long list of skills with no differentiation
- No metrics anywhere on the page
- Skills section that lists everything the candidate has ever touched

## How to stand out

Use the outcome formula for every bullet:

> Accomplished **[X]** as measured by **[Y]** by doing **[Z]**.

**Before:** *Contributed to transforming our app into a multi-module app.*

**After:** *Transformed our Android application into a multi-module app by introducing dynamic feature modules, lowering build times by 35%.*

Strong action verbs: Acquired · Coordinated · Formalized · Launched · Orchestrated · Spearheaded · Transformed · Yielded · Sustained · Developed

Write a summary statement that describes what you offer, not what you want.

## AI-assisted prep

- Paste a bullet point and ask for a rewrite using the X/Y/Z formula
- Ask AI to identify which bullets are activity-based vs. outcome-based
- Paste the job description alongside your résumé and ask which requirements are not yet represented
- Generate a stronger summary statement from your existing experience section
