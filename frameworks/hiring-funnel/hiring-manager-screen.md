# Hiring Manager Screen

## Goal of this phase

Start to establish a relationship. The evaluation shifts here from qualification to fit. The manager is asking: *Is this someone I can work with? Do they understand what this role actually requires?*

## Audience

Your potential direct manager. They have read your résumé and passed you through. Now they want to hear how you think, talk, and carry yourself. This is the first human-to-human signal in the funnel.

## What the reviewer is looking for

- Can you tell your own story clearly and with intent?
- Do you demonstrate the capabilities the role requires — in your own words?
- Are you someone who would work well with this team?
- Do you understand what this role is actually asking for?

## Visible signals

- How you answer "Tell me about yourself"
- Whether your examples are specific and relevant to this role
- How you talk about past teams, managers, and challenges
- Whether you ask real questions

## Invisible signals

- Whether you have thought about *why this role* — not just that you want a job
- How you frame difficult situations — do you take ownership or assign blame?
- Judgment about what to include and what to leave out of your story
- Whether your narrative arc covers what the job description actually requires

## Common mistakes

- Reciting your résumé chronologically instead of telling a story
- Not connecting your experience to the specific requirements of this role
- Saying things that undermine trust (speaking negatively about past employers, overstating impact)
- Not asking any questions — or asking questions that could have been answered by reading the job description

## How to stand out

Prepare a "Tell me about yourself" answer that follows this arc:

1. Where you have been — briefly, with a thread of intent
2. What you have built or led — one concrete, relevant example
3. Why this role, at this company, now — specific and honest

Your story should cover the job requirements without sounding like you are reading them back.

Ask questions that reveal you have thought about the role:

- What does success look like in the first six months?
- How would you describe your leadership style?
- What is the rest of the team like right now?
- What does a typical week look like in this role?

## AI-assisted prep

- Draft a "Tell me about yourself" answer from your résumé and the job description
- Identify which job requirements are not covered in your current narrative and suggest ways to address them
- Generate likely hiring manager questions for this role type and level
- Review your answers for blame language or vague ownership framing
