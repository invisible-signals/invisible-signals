# Recruiter Screen

## Goal of this phase

Show that you are a plausible fit for the role and company. The recruiter is determining whether it is worth the hiring team's time to continue — this is a compatibility check, not a technical evaluation.

## Audience

In-house recruiter or external recruiting partner. They are not evaluating technical depth. They are pattern-matching against the role requirements and assessing whether you are worth routing to the next stage.

## What the reviewer is looking for

- Basic qualifications match the role
- Compensation expectations are in range
- You have done some research on the company
- You seem genuinely interested, not just volume-applying

## Visible signals

- How you describe your background and experience
- Whether you can state a compensation range clearly
- Whether you ask any questions about the role or team
- Your communication style and pace

## Invisible signals

- Preparation level — did you read anything about the company before this call?
- Genuine vs. performative interest — one specific observation about the company signals more than generic enthusiasm
- Whether you are easy to work with as a candidate (responsive, clear, direct)

## Common mistakes

- Not researching the company before the call
- Having no comp range ready, or anchoring to personal need instead of market data
- Generic answers that could apply to any company
- Not asking any questions
- Treating the recruiter screen as unimportant and underpreparing

## How to stand out

- Come with one informed observation about the company (from their blog, recent news, or product)
- Know your comp range before the call — use market data like [levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi) to anchor it
- Ask one real question about the team, the role, or what success looks like in the first 90 days
- Be direct — recruiters move fast and appreciate clarity over polish

## AI-assisted prep

- Summarize a company's About Us page, recent blog posts, or press to prepare a specific observation
- Generate likely recruiter screen questions for a given role and practice answering them
- Draft a comp range narrative: how to state your number confidently without over-explaining
- Identify what makes this role different from similar ones and why that distinction matters to you
