# Boundary Setting

## Goal of this pattern

Coaching engineers and leaders to define clear boundaries for support, communicate them proactively, and transition responsibility back to where it belongs — protecting their contribution signal and their time.

## Core pattern

Support can drift into shaping other teams' artifacts or absorbing other teams' responsibilities, burning precious cycles. The coaching principle is to define clear boundaries for support, communicate them proactively, and transition responsibility back where it belongs.

This protects signal integrity — contribution should be real, visible, and sustainable.

## When this shows up

- The person is always "helping" but cannot describe what they own
- Other teams' work regularly ends up on their plate without an explicit decision
- Their output is spread across many initiatives with no concentrated signal
- They are seen as available rather than as an owner

## How to use in 1:1s

- **What work are you carrying that should belong to someone else?**
- **Where are you being helpful in a way that hides an ownership problem?**
- **Coach toward: support without silent adoption of responsibility.**

## Signal Stack connection

- **Layer 3 — Ownership**: Is contribution real, visible, and sustainable — or diffuse and difficult to attribute?
- **Layer 8 — Leadership Maturity**: Does the person protect their cycles and signal, or optimize for availability?
