# Decision Rights Clarity

## Goal of this pattern

Teaching decision-rights clarity as a career skill — helping engineers and leaders become more effective and more legible as leaders by clarifying ownership, DRI assignment, escalation paths, and approval boundaries.

## Core pattern

A major pattern across growing technical organizations is ambiguity around ownership, decision-making, DRI assignment, escalation paths, and approval boundaries. That is not just an org problem — it is a career-coaching opportunity. People who can clarify ownership and decision rights become more effective and more legible as leaders.

The communication standard from the repo applies here directly: communication is part of engineering effectiveness.

## When this shows up

- Cross-team work is stalling without a clear driver
- The person cannot name who owns a decision they are involved in
- Approval churn or rework is coming from unclear ownership
- Ambiguity is described as "just how things are here"

## How to use in 1:1s

- **What decision do you think you own that others may think they own too?**
- **Where is ambiguity costing you time or trust?**
- Coach them to leave every cross-team discussion able to state:
  - the decision
  - the DRI
  - the escalation path
  - the handoff boundary

That is career leverage, not just project hygiene.

## Signal Stack connection

- **Layer 4 — Communication**: Can they make complex ownership and decision structures understandable?
- **Layer 7 — Strategic Thinking**: Do they understand how decision rights affect delivery velocity?
