# Coordination vs Ownership

## Goal of this pattern

Coaching someone out of coordination-only work so they build a delivery track record with clear attribution.

## Core pattern

Growth stalls when someone is doing mostly coordination work without clear delivery ownership. The coaching action is to create a proof-point project with clear scope, partner on end-to-end delivery, and ensure the person is demonstrating technical leadership — not just orchestration.

This maps to the repo's core thesis: strong career systems should make real experience easier to understand, not inflate it.

## When this shows up

- The person's impact sounds like a list of meetings and handoffs
- Work is described as "helping," "supporting," or "coordinating" with no named outcome they fully own
- There is no single deliverable from the last quarter the person can point to
- Trajectory has stalled despite visible activity

## How to use in 1:1s

- **What work are you truly owning end-to-end — not just coordinating?**
- **What proof-point project would make your impact obvious in 60–90 days?**
- Push for one visible outcome with named scope, measurable result, and clear attribution.

## Signal Stack connection

- **Layer 3 — Ownership**: Is this person driving outcomes or facilitating them?
- **Layer 2 — Execution Reliability**: Can they follow through on delivery, not just coordination?

Coordination-only work produces no Signal Stack™ evidence an interviewer or promotion committee can read.
