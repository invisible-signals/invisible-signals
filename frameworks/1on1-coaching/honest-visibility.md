# Honest Visibility

## Goal of this pattern

Helping people translate what they did into clear, honest signal — what they owned, what improved, what risk they reduced, what system they clarified, and what they learned.

## Core pattern

Leadership visibility should be earned through consistent contribution, not self-promotion theater. A strong pattern in 1:1 coaching is helping people translate recent work into credible evidence — what they owned, what improved, what risk they reduced, what system they clarified, and what they learned.

This keeps coaching grounded and avoids inflated narratives.

## When this shows up

- The person describes their value but cannot point to evidence
- Their impact requires lengthy explanation to sound significant
- They are selling rather than showing
- Their narrative outpaces what the record actually reflects

## How to use in 1:1s

- **What is the most credible evidence of your growth right now?**
- **If someone senior looked only at your last 60 days, what signal would they see?**
- **What contribution have you made that improved clarity, speed, or quality for the team?**

## Signal Stack connection

- **Layer 4 — Communication**: Can they translate real work into understandable signal?
- **Layer 8 — Leadership Maturity**: Are they building sustainable visibility or short-term narrative?
