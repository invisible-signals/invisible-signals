# Diagnose, Not Monitor

## Goal of this pattern

Coaching technical leaders to replace status-collecting behaviors with diagnostic depth — drilling into blockers, applying judgment, and offering concrete paths forward.

## Core pattern

A recurring leadership failure is asking for status without drilling into blockers, and offering options instead of helping solve the problem. The coaching action is to balance delegation with more technical depth and provide diagnostic support — not just escalation pathways.

This builds the kind of judgment and problem-solving signal that hiring committees and promotion panels value.

## When this shows up

- Status updates replace problem-solving in 1:1s and team meetings
- The leader can describe what is blocked but cannot explain why
- The person escalates before trying to unblock
- Technical depth has atrophied because of over-delegation

## How to use in 1:1s

- **Where are you reviewing progress instead of helping unblock it?**
- **What issue this week required deeper technical judgment from you?**
- **Coach toward: "Show me how you debugged the ambiguity."**

## Signal Stack connection

- **Layer 2 — Execution Reliability**: Does this person resolve blockers or report them?
- **Layer 8 — Leadership Maturity**: Do they make the system better or just navigate it?

Leaders who only monitor do not accumulate problem-solving signal. That gap becomes visible in interviews and performance reviews.
