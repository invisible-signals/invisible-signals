# Blockers to Leadership Signal

## Goal of this pattern

Teaching people to convert blockers into artifacts of leadership — risk framing, decision framing, dependency maps, and proposed paths forward — rather than just reporting friction.

## Core pattern

Careers are shaped by how signals are interpreted. A strong coaching move is to teach people that how they respond to blockers becomes part of their career signal. Blockers are not just obstacles — they are leadership opportunities. The person who names the problem clearly, proposes a path forward, and creates a decision-ready artifact is showing judgment and ownership.

## When this shows up

- The person describes what is wrong without proposing what to do about it
- Blockers are reported in meetings but not reduced through artifacts
- Ambiguity is escalated intact instead of being structured first
- The person is seen as a reporter of problems, not a solver of them

## How to use in 1:1s

- **What blocker did you convert into a concrete recommendation this week?**
- **What ambiguity did you reduce for others — not just surface?**
- **What artifact did you create that made the work easier to understand?**

## Signal Stack connection

- **Layer 4 — Communication**: Are problems communicated in a way that moves work forward?
- **Layer 6 — Collaboration and Influence**: Does the person move work through people and systems, or just report friction?
