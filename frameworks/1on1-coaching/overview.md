# 1:1 Coaching Overview

## Purpose

Career coaching through signal clarity. Not generic development advice. Not performance theater. Helping engineers and technical leaders:

- own something real
- solve something hard
- clarify boundaries
- and leave behind visible evidence of impact

This framework is useful for both managers coaching their reports and for individuals using these principles to assess their own growth.

---

## The 4-part coaching lens

Use this as a consistent lens across 1:1s to evaluate career signal at any level.

| Lens | Core question |
| --- | --- |
| **Proof of ownership** | What are you truly driving end-to-end? |
| **Problem-solving depth** | Where did you personally reduce ambiguity or unblock something hard? |
| **Boundary clarity** | What do you own — and what should you stop absorbing? |
| **Visible signal** | What artifact or outcome shows your impact clearly and honestly? |

---

## Drop-in 1:1 questions

These questions work for managers coaching their reports and for individuals reflecting on their own growth.

- What are you owning end-to-end right now — not just coordinating?
- Where do you need to go one level deeper technically instead of staying at status level?
- What ambiguity did you remove this week for the team?
- What responsibility are you carrying that should be pushed back to the right owner?
- What proof point would best demonstrate the next level of your growth?
- What is the clearest evidence of your value that does not require explanation?

---

## Signal Stack connection

This coaching framework maps directly to the Signal Stack™:

- **Proof of ownership** → Layer 3: Ownership
- **Problem-solving depth** → Layer 2: Execution Reliability + Layer 8: Leadership Maturity
- **Boundary clarity** → Layer 3: Ownership + Layer 7: Strategic Thinking
- **Visible signal** → Layer 4: Communication + Layer 8: Leadership Maturity
