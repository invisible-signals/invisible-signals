## Philosophy

Invisible Signals is not designed to help candidates game interviews.

It is designed to help engineers and leaders communicate their actual value more clearly.

We believe:

- hiring is fundamentally signal interpretation
- communication is part of engineering effectiveness
- AI should amplify clarity, not fabricate competence
- strong career systems outperform reactive job searching
