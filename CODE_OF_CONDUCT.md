# Code of Conduct

Invisible Signals™ is intended to be a thoughtful, practical space for improving how technical people communicate their work. Participation should reflect that purpose.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still being accountable for impact.
- Give feedback that is specific, actionable, and kind.
- Welcome people with different backgrounds, communication styles, and levels of experience.
- Keep discussion focused on improving the project and its users' outcomes.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions after being asked to stop.
- Encouraging deception, fabrication, or unethical hiring behavior.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainer privately with relevant context and links when possible.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its community.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, and community channels associated with Invisible Signals™.
